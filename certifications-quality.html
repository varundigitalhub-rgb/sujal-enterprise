Zero-Defect Goal

ISO 9001, 45001 & PED Certified Quality

At Sujal Enterprise, quality is our foundation. We are proud to be ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management), ISO 45001:2018 (Occupational Health & Safety), and PED (Pressure Equipment Directive) Certified, reflecting our commitment to international quality and safety standards.

Our quality management system ensures all sourced Stainless Steel (SS), Mild Steel (MS), and GI Fittings are audited for dimensional accuracy, material purity, and compliance with relevant standards before listing. Every product meets the specified mechanical and chemical properties.

Every dispatch includes fully traceable material test certificates, ensuring complete transparency and quality assurance for our clients across India.

Testing Capabilities

  • Chemical Analysis (Spectroscopy) Verifies alloy percentage (Nickel, Chrome, Moly, Carbon).
  • Mechanical & Hardness Testing Verifies tensile yield limits and material hardness.
  • Hydrostatic Testing High pressure leak tests for pipes and seamless bends.
Inspection Workflow

Our Quality Control Workflow

1. Incoming Material Audits

Before stocking, raw steel billets, seamless pipes, and forged components are inspected for surface cracks, laminations, and dimensional deviations.

2. In-Process Testing

Dimensional inspections are performed during threading, beveling, and custom size forging runs to ensure tolerances strictly align with ASME specifications.

3. Pre-Shipment Markings

Approved products are heat-marked with the batch code, size, rating, and material grade. A final review ensures corresponding test certificates are bundled.
