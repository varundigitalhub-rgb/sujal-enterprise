Zero-Defect Goal

Uncompromising Quality Standards

At Sujal Enterprise, quality control is not just a stage in our warehouse—it is the foundation of our business model.

Our quality management system ensures all sourced raw pipes, flanges, and high-pressure elbow parts are audited for dimensional accuracy and alloy purity before listing. We verify that pressure-carrying capacities meet design margins, preventing premature on-site pipeline failures.

Every dispatch includes fully traceable Mill Test Certificates (MTC) matching EN 10204 3.1 specifications. These documents outline the exact chemical and physical characteristics of the batch.

Testing Capabilities

  • Chemical Analysis (Spectroscopy) Verifies alloy percentage (Nickel, Chrome, Moly, Carbon).
  • Mechanical & Hardness Testing Verifies tensile yield limits and material hardness.
  • Hydrostatic Testing High pressure leak tests for pipes and seamless bends.
Inspection Workflow

Our Quality Control Workflow

1. Incoming Material Audits

Before stocking, raw steel billets, seamless pipes, and forged components are inspected for surface cracks, laminations, and dimensional deviations.

2. In-Process Testing

Dimensional inspections are performed during threading, beveling, and custom size forging runs to ensure tolerances strictly align with ASME specifications.

3. Pre-Shipment Markings

Approved products are heat-marked with the batch code, size, rating, and material grade. A final review ensures corresponding test certificates are bundled.

Compliance Standards We Meet

ASME / ANSI B16.11 Forged / B16.9 Buttweld / B16.5 Flanges
ASTM Standards A105 / A106 / A312 TP304 & TP316 Alloys
EN Standards EN 10204 3.1 Certified Material Traceability
TPI Support Verified inspections by SGS, TUV, Lloyds, and ABS
