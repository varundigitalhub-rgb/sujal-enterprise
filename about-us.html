Corporate Profile

An ISO 9001, 45001 & PED Certified Enterprise

Founded at Bharat Lokhand Bazaar, Mumbai's premier metal trading hub, Sujal Enterprise has established itself as a trusted manufacturer, supplier, exporter, and stockist of premium quality Stainless Steel (SS), Mild Steel (MS), and GI Fittings.

As an ISO 9001, 45001 & PED Certified organization, we are committed to delivering certified industrial products with full material traceability. Our product range includes SS sheets, plates, coils, pipes, pipe fittings, flanges, round bars, and valves; MS pipes, plates, sheets, and coils; and a comprehensive range of GI fittings including elbows, unions, tees, couplings, and socket fittings.

Whether coordinating bulk supply for large infrastructure projects or fulfilling custom specifications for specialized industrial applications, we ensure every product meets rigorous quality standards.
