Corporate Profile

An ISO 9001, 45001 & PED Certified Enterprise

Founded at Bharat Lokhand Bazaar, Mumbai's premier metal trading hub, Sujal Enterprise has established itself as a trusted manufacturer, supplier, and stockist of premium industrial steel products in Stainless Steel, Mild Steel, Alloy Steel, and IBR.

As an ISO 9001, 45001 & PED Certified organization, we are committed to delivering certified industrial products with full material traceability. Our product range includes Flanges, Pipes, Round Bars, Sheets, Plates, Buttweld Fittings, Forged Fittings, and Channel & Angle, available in Stainless Steel, Mild Steel, Alloy Steel and IBR grades.

Whether coordinating bulk supply for large infrastructure projects or fulfilling custom specifications for specialized industrial applications, we ensure every product meets rigorous quality standards and we serve clients across India with prompt deliveries and competitive pricing.
