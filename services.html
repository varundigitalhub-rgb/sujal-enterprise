Industrial Value-Added Services

Sujal Enterprise offers customized services for Stainless Steel (SS), Mild Steel (MS), and GI Fittings to meet project-specific requirements. ISO 9001, 45001 & PED Certified.

Custom Size Forging

We coordinate forging of carbon and stainless steel flanges and socket-weld fittings to non-standard sizing constraints. Our partners forge raw billets matching chemical composition sheets.

  • Custom flange diameters
  • High-temp alloy fittings
  • Special pressure classes
  • Trace-tested raw steel

Precision Metal Cutting

We offer exact-dimensional cutting for mild steel round pipes, carbon structural tubes, and stainless steel sheets, eliminating scrap loss at your construction site.

  • Bandsaw pipe cutting
  • High-tolerance chamfering
  • Beveled edge preparation
  • Custom sheet sizing

Pipe Threading & Grooving

Providing high-speed threading and groove rolling for fire fighting lines, water pipes, and plumbing lines matching NPT, BSPT, or grooved couplings.

  • NPT Thread standards
  • BSPT Taper threads
  • Victaulic grooving specs
  • Anti-rust end capping

Wholesale Logistics & Supply

We coordinate bundled wholesale pipeline materials directly with domestic road carriers and global dispatch ports. Keeping packaging clean and inventory labeled.

  • Bundled pipe strapping
  • Wooden crate packing
  • Port-customs logistics
  • Labeled dispatch codes
