Who We Are

Engineered Steel Solutions for Critical Infrastructure

Powering India's Industrial Growth Since 2017

Headquartered in Mumbai, SUJAL ENTERPRISE has established itself as a trusted manufacturer, supplier and stockist of precision-engineered industrial steel products. Since 2017, we have been delivering high-quality steel solutions to industries across India through our expertise in manufacturing, strategic inventory management and reliable supply.

Our product portfolio includes Flanges, Pipes, Round Bars, Sheets, Plates, Buttweld Fittings, Forged Fittings and Channel & Angle, available in Mild Steel, Stainless Steel, Alloy Steel and IBR grades to meet diverse industrial requirements.

As an ISO 9001, ISO 45001 and PED Certified organization, we are committed to delivering products that meet international quality standards, ensuring dependable performance, competitive pricing and on-time delivery for every project.
